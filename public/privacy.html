Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

NSBE Concordia operates the NSBE Concordia Membership application to manage chapter membership and support chapter activities. This policy explains what information the application collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you submit the membership form, we collect your name, email address, phone number if provided, membership date, and membership status. Chapter officers may also create or update records related to events, attendance, newsletters, and job postings.

How we use information

  • To maintain the NSBE Concordia membership list.
  • To communicate with members about chapter activities, events, and opportunities.
  • To record event attendance and produce chapter administration reports.
  • To manage newsletters and relevant job postings.
  • To protect and operate the application.

Google services and storage

Membership and chapter administration data is stored in Google Sheets controlled by NSBE Concordia. The application uses Google OAuth to authorize access to those sheets. Google account information used during authorization is used to identify the authorized chapter officer. We do not use Google account data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Access and sharing

Access to member administration features is limited to the configured chapter officer account. We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only when required by law or when necessary to operate the application and its storage services.

Retention and deletion

We retain membership information while it is needed for chapter administration. You may request correction or deletion of your information by contacting NSBE Concordia. Chapter records may be retained when needed for legitimate administrative or legal purposes.

Security

We use access controls, authenticated administration, HTTPS in production, and restricted server-side credentials to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the NSBE Concordia chapter through its official chapter contact channel.
